Quiz: Part III

Six questions on the chapters of Part III written so far. More will be added with the remaining chapters.

Choose an answer to see whether it is right and why. Your best score is kept in this browser only.

  1. The weight that Sz(x) gives to a prime power n ≤ x is:

    • Λ(n)
    • Λ(n)/√n
    • Λ(n) · (x/n − 1)
    • Λ(n) · ln(x/n)

    The weight falls to 0 at n = x, so Sz is continuous where ψ jumps. Equivalently Sz(x) = x · ∫1x ψ(t)/t² dt.

  2. What does the smoothing do to the term of a zero at height γ?

    • Removes it
    • Changes its amplitude from about 2/γ to about 2/γ², so the sum over zeros converges absolutely
    • Doubles its frequency
    • Moves it onto the critical line

    xρ/ρ becomes xρ/(ρ(ρ − 1)). The sum of 2/(¼ + γ²) over all zeros is about 0.0462, a finite number, whereas the sum of 2/γ diverges.

  3. If the Riemann Hypothesis holds, (Gz(x) − ln 2π)/√x stays within about:

    • ±0.0462
    • ±1
    • ±ln x
    • ±0.5

    That is the sum of the amplitudes of all the waves. The first zero alone accounts for 0.0100 of it.

  4. The formalization proves only an upper bound on the smoothed error. Why is that enough?

    • The lower bound is obvious
    • The error is always positive
    • Lean cannot express lower bounds
    • By a theorem of Landau, a zero off the line forces the error to exceed the bound in both directions

    A zero with real part β > θ contributes an oscillating term of size xβ that takes both signs, and Landau's theorem shows the other terms cannot cancel it. So a one-sided bound C · xθ already excludes such zeros.

  5. A hypothetical zero with β = 0.55 would make its wave larger than an on-line wave by a factor of x0.05. At x = 20,000 that factor is about:

    • 20
    • 141
    • 1.6
    • 1.05

    20,0000.05 = e0.05 · 9.9 ≈ 1.6. This is why no computation over a feasible range of x can settle the hypothesis: an off-line zero close to the line shows itself only at enormous x.

  6. In a Lean file, sorry means:

    • The statement is false
    • The proof of that statement is missing
    • The statement is an axiom of Mathlib
    • The proof was checked by hand

    Lean accepts sorry in place of any proof and reports that it did so. A development is complete only when none remain and the final theorem depends on no extra axioms.
