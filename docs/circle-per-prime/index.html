What the paper does with this

The paper works in the completed spaces of chapter 16. It splits the of the into one piece for each prime outside a finite set SSS, plus a remainder; SSS depends on the test function and on the character. For the pieces outside SSS it shows that the along the diagonal is 0. The remainder involves only the finitely many primes in SSS and the real numbers, and is computed with a version of an existing result, the "S-local " of and . The paper notes that the non-vanishing of the on the line Re⁡s=1\operatorname{Re} s = 1Res=1, a classical theorem, is used as an input.

The lab shows the arithmetic that the construction is designed to reproduce. It does not test the construction: the splitting of the kernel and the vanishing of the pieces are statements about operators on the completed spaces, and they are where the paper's new work lies.
