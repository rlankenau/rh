Companion site

Two claimed proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis, one day

On 20 November 2026 two unrelated proofs of the generalized Riemann Hypothesis were released. This site works through both, one step at a time, with labs you can play with: every figure is computed in your browser from the definitions, and the sliders change the actual functions under discussion.

Neither proof has been verified, and nothing here says either is correct. These are a reader's notes: each chapter says what it is based on and how closely that source was read.

How to use it

  • The three background chapters are shared by both proofs. Read them first, in order.
  • Each proof has a one-page map of the whole argument, then chapters that take one step each. Chapters marked planned are not written yet.
  • Each chapter ends with a few things to try in its lab.
