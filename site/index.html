Two claimed proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis, one day

On 20 November 2026 two unrelated proofs of the generalized Riemann Hypothesis were released. These are a reader's maps of each: how the argument is organized, what has been checked, and what is missing. Neither proof has been verified, and nothing here says either is correct.

Alpöge with Claude

A Lean 4 formalization of 175,187 files with no accompanying paper. RH and GRH for Dirichlet L-functions, by a chain of theta lifts and a one-sided bound on a prime sum. kernel check in progress

Masullo

“The Music of the Primes”, a 940-page paper. GRH for Hecke L-functions of every number field, by a trace formula on a space that only registers zeros near the critical line. incomplete manuscript
Notes, the gap scan, and small Lean experiments are in the repository. Written with Claude.
